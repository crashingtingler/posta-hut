Delete your Posta hut data

The quick way: in the game

Open Settings and untick Online Hall of Masters. Your entry in the online Hall (name, XP, best streak and fencer) is deleted straight away. Everything else is only on your device and goes when you uninstall.

If you no longer have the game

Ask us here and we'll delete your online entry within 30 days. Several players can share a name, so tell us anything that helps us find yours.

What is deleted

Your online Hall entry and any reports linked to it. We keep nothing else about you. See the privacy policy.
