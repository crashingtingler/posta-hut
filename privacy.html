Posta hut · Privacy

Effective 20 November 2026

Posta hut is a free game for learning the guards of historical European martial arts. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your email, phone number, location or contacts.

What stays on your device

Your player names, XP, best streaks, Armoury gear, settings and review deck are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the website's data) removes them.

The online Hall of Masters

So that players can see each other in the Hall of Masters, the game sends the following to our database, hosted by Supabase in the United States:

WhatWhy
The display name you chooseShown in the Hall
Your total XP and best streakTo rank the Hall
Your fencer's look (garb, armour, weapon, guard, colours)To draw your fencer in the Hall
A random player IDSo only you can change your own entry. It is not linked to your name, email or device.

Your name, XP, streak and fencer are public: anyone playing can see them. Supabase's servers also keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as most web services do.

If you report another player's name, we keep that report (your player ID, theirs, and the name) so we can review it.

Reporting an error

If you use Report an error, we receive what you write, the name or email you choose to add (optional), and your device's browser details (to help fix the problem). Reports are delivered by Formspree.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → untick Online Hall of Masters. Your online entry is deleted at once.
  • If you no longer have the game: use the deletion request page.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Children

Posta hut is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children: the only thing you type that others see is your display name, which is checked against a filter, and any name can be reported.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Send them through Report an error in the game's Settings, or through the request page.
